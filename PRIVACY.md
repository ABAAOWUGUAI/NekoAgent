# Privacy boundary

The platform stores Assistant, conversation, task, delivery and learning state
in the deployer's own runtime storage. Configure retention and backups for your
deployment.

When a deployer binds a third-party model Provider, the messages and approved
media sent to that Provider are also governed by that Provider's terms. Model
catalog discovery does not authorize a Provider or prove that a particular
model is usable for the deployer's account.

The platform does not package another operator's conversations, credentials,
QQ account state, media or private Assistant identity. Third-party AstrBot
plugins and Skills have their own trust boundaries; install them only after
review and explicit approval.
